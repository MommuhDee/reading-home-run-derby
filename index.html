Games, progress & backups

How to play and save the app

In Family Practice, one group takes turns with up to six children. In Two-Team Competition, each team has its own batting order. After a hit, the next child on the same team bats. Teams switch automatically after the selected at-bats per child or three outs, whichever comes first. You can also switch manually. Select a single, double, triple, or home run reading challenge. Let them sound out and reread carefully, then award the hit. Outs are optional baseball events, never penalties for slow reading. Three outs end the side early, clear the bases, and switch teams or advance the practice inning. The next teammate bats automatically after each hit, and the runners stay on base until the team switches sides.

Scores are stored on this device in this browser, not in a shared online account. Your friends' scores are separate from yours. Use Backup scores regularly, especially before updates. To add the app to an Android home screen, open the hosted GitHub Pages site in Chrome and select Add to Home screen or Install app.
